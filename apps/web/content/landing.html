Questions

What teams ask before they install.

Email us

Does PullPanda keep my code?

No. The worker fetches the diff from GitHub for the review and stores only the findings and token counts.

Does PullPanda read the whole repository?

No. It reads the changed lines and the lines around them, so it can miss a problem that only shows across files.

What starts a review?

Opening, reopening or marking a pull request ready, and every push to it.

Can I turn reviews off for one repository?

Yes. Switch it off in the dashboard, or commit enabled: false in .pullpanda.yml.

Which model writes the reviews?

An OpenAI model chosen by whoever runs PullPanda. Its answer is checked against the diff, and comments on lines outside it are dropped.

How do I cancel a Pro subscription?

Cancel in the billing portal. Pro stays on for that repository until the paid month ends.
